# Mode: training -- Course and Certification Evaluation

For every course or certification the candidate asks about, evaluate 6
dimensions:

| Dimension | What it evaluates |
|-----------|-------------------|
| North Star alignment | Does it move the candidate closer to or further from the target? |
| Recruiter signal | What will hiring managers think when they see this on a CV? |
| Time and effort | Weeks x hours per week |
| Opportunity cost | What will the candidate not be able to do during that time? |
| Risks | Outdated content? Weak brand? Too basic? |
| Portfolio deliverable | Does it produce a demonstrable artifact? |

## Verdicts

- **DO** -- 4-12 week plan with weekly deliverables and scoreboard
- **DO NOT DO** -- better alternative with justification
- **DO WITH A TIMEBOX** (max X weeks) -- condensed plan, essentials only

## Priority

Training that improves credibility in "production-grade AI":

1. LLM evals and testing
2. Observability and monitoring
3. Cost/reliability trade-offs
4. AI governance and safety
5. Enterprise AI architecture
